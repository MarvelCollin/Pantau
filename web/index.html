Pantau, a live map of Indonesia

Follow flights, ships, satellites, earthquakes, volcano alert levels, fire hotspots, flood reports, rain radar and public road cameras across Indonesia, refreshed from free public data.

Peta langsung Indonesia: pesawat, kapal, satelit, gempa bumi BMKG, status gunung api, titik api, laporan banjir, radar hujan dan CCTV jalan.

  • Flights from ADS-B receivers through adsb.lol
  • Ships from AIS through aisstream.io
  • Earthquakes from BMKG and USGS
  • Volcano alert levels from MAGMA ESDM
  • Fire hotspots from NASA FIRMS
  • Flood and haze reports from PetaBencana
  • Rain radar from RainViewer and weather from Open-Meteo
  • Satellites from CelesTrak
  • Road cameras from Bina Marga and LTA Singapore
